The house

A garden restaurant on Kabula Hill.

Late afternoon is quiet up here. The terrace faces the slope toward Michiru, the oven and the grill run together, and the dining room keeps a lower light once the sun drops.

The kitchen grills fillet, T-bone, and rib-eye with baby roast potatoes, bakes thin-crust pizzas, and, Thursday to Sunday, puts out a chocolate cheesecake and a lotus biscuit cheesecake. Milkshakes are a house habit. The lawn also hosts pop-up markets and private gatherings. A few rooms sit on the same grounds, if you would rather walk to dinner.
